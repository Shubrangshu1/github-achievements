# Galaxy Brain Achievement Guide

The **Galaxy Brain** badge is awarded to developers whose answers are marked as accepted in GitHub Discussions.

## Tiers
- **Bronze**: 2 accepted answers
- **Silver**: 8 accepted answers
- **Gold**: 16 accepted answers
- **Onyx**: 32 accepted answers

## Key Requirements
- Must be in a public repository with Discussions Q&A enabled.
- The answer must be marked by the discussion creator or maintainer.
